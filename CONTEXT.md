# Trading Workbench

A personal trade journal and futures market analysis workspace.

## Language

**Analysis run**:
A single requested market or journal analysis, including its progress and eventual result or failure. Leaving the page does not cancel the run.

**Market analysis**:
An assessment of observed prices for one dated contract using the user's stated position. It produces a recommendation and does not place orders.

**Journal analysis**:
A retrospective assessment of saved closed trades selected by the user's journal filters.
